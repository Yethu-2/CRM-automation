Meetings & triggers

A client meeting triggers DeepSeek to draft CRM updates from the transcript and that client's Pipedrive records. Only meetings indexed to a client can be triggered, so drafts never fall back to whichever account is connected.

Review queue

Nothing reaches Pipedrive without a decision here, and this app never writes to Pipedrive itself: an approved draft becomes a change plan for a person to apply. Untick changes you don't want before approving. Redo sends your feedback back to DeepSeek for a new revision.
