# Komosion commercial assessment: the 12 criteria

Source: Phase 2 handover (Natthaporn Wanna, "Data Contract Handover", 22 Oct 2026), section 3, built with John's
feedback. This file exists because Phase 2 testing found models confusing these with the recommendation-evaluator's
12 acceptance criteria. When asked for "the 12 criteria" in a commercial sense, use these.

| Domain | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Opportunity reality | 1. Defined business problem · 2. Supporting budget / funding · 3. Client intent / commitment |
| Relationship & authority | 4. Relationship access / referral quality · 5. Decision mandate / authority |
| Commercial environment | 6. Competitive pressure · 7. Opportunity cost · 8. Commercial / profitability potential |
| Delivery reality | 9. Brief / scope clarity · 10. Timing and delivery feasibility · 11. Expectation realism · 12. Capability / talent access |

## For each criterion, give
- the assessment;
- supporting evidence IDs (Pipedrive record IDs, document or transcript references);
- whether it is a **fact** or an **inference**;
- confidence;
- information gaps, and risk flags where they apply.

**Unknown is not a negative score.** Record it as a gap.

## Posture
Suggest one of: **Proceed / Qualify further / Research required / Hold–nurture / Decline**, with the reasons.
The human makes the final commercial decision.

## John's priorities
A defined problem with credible budget; high-quality referral or access; mandate; realistic expectations and
timelines; competition; opportunity cost; delivery capability and profitable execution. Don't present
comparison-only interest or tyre-kicking as confirmed without supporting evidence.
