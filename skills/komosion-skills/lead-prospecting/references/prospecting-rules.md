# Prospecting rules (to be completed by the team before first run)

1. **Approved sources**: e.g. company websites, official registries, trade bodies, conference exhibitor lists, public news. List them explicitly. Exclude scraped personal profiles and purchased lists unless legal review approves.
2. **Contact data**: business-published roles and addresses only. No guessing or pattern-generating addresses.
3. **Regional rules**: record which privacy and outreach rules apply to each target region (e.g. GDPR in the EU, local data-protection law in the target country) and who confirmed them. This file is guidance, not legal advice.
4. **Volume caps and review**: candidates per run, who reviews, review turnaround.
5. **Destination**: leads inbox with source and evidence attached; deals only after a human converts a lead.
6. **Retention**: how long unapproved candidates are kept.
